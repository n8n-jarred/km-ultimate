Program 01 · Fundamentals & Field Awareness

Beginner Program

Target Audience: New players, athletes transitioning from other sports, or those with zero frisbee exposure.

Core Objective: Build consistent throw/catch mechanics, understand fundamental field space, and learn self-officiating.

M1

Throwing Mechanics & Grip Work

▼
  • Backhand: Stance, pivot foot placement, pulling across the chest, and flat wrist-snap release.
  • Forehand (Flick): Middle-finger rim grip, leading with the elbow, wrist-whip motion, and eliminating disc wobble.
  • Hammer (Basic introduction): Upside-down grip and overhead release for high-floating passes.
📚 References
Beginner's Playbook — Throwing Basics Video: How To Throw A Backhand (Brodie Smith) Video: Forehand Flick Tutorial (Kurt Gibson) Ultimate 101 Series (UFA)
M2

Catching & Receiving

▼
  • Pancake (Clap) Catch: Two-handed top/bottom sandwich for low to chest-high discs.
  • Rim Catch: Two-handed rim tracking for high or wide passes.
  • Attacking the Disc: Sprinting toward the throw instead of waiting for it to arrive.
📚 References
Playbook — Catching Basics Video: Pancake (Clap) Catch (Brodie Smith) Video: Two-Handed Rim Catch (USA Ultimate)
M3

Field Positioning & Basic Offense

▼
  • Vertical Stack (Vert Stack): Understanding the central line, active cutter positions, and handler resets.
  • Basic Cutting Rules: Clearing out space, making sharp 45-degree cuts, and avoiding clog points.
  • Dump & Reset: Executing 5-yard backward/lateral drop passes when the stall count reaches 6.
📚 References
Video: How To Cut in Ultimate — 9 Beginner Tips Video: Pivoting & Faking (KC Ultimate)
M4

Defensive Basics & Rules

▼
  • Individual Marking: Force side (Home/Away or Backhand/Forehand force), maintaining legal contact distance, and active stall counting (1 to 10).
  • Person-to-Person (Man) Defense: Positioning between the cutter and the thrower, looking at the belly button, not the disc.
  • Rules & Spirit of the Game (SOTG): Self-officiating, calling fouls/travels/picks, and resolving disputes with mutual respect.

Program 02 · Spatial Intelligence & Tactical Mechanics

Intermediate Program

Target Audience: Club-level players, league regulars, and athletes with 1–2 years of structured play.

Core Objective: Master variable throwing angles, fluid movement patterns, and adaptive team defense.

M1

Advanced Throwing & Break-Marking

▼
  • Throwing Variations: Inside-out (IO) and Outside-in (OI) releases for both forehand and backhand.
  • Break-Marking: Stepping around a mark to throw to the closed side of the field using low-release fakes.
  • Wind Adjustments: Reading wind direction (headwind, tailwind, crosswind) and adjusting disc angle/edge accordingly.
📚 References
Video: Backhand & Flick Masterclass (cchanulti) Video: Adding Touch to Your Throws — 3 Drills (Rowan McDonnell)
M2

Defensive Structures

▼
  • Zone Defenses: Understanding roles in a 3-3-1 or 4-2-1 structure (Cup/Mark, Short Deep, Wings, Deep Deep).
  • Switching & Bracket Defense: Communication protocols for passing off cutters in high-risk field sectors.
  • Flash Defenses: Generating unexpected turnovers by sagging off non-threatening handlers to clog lane options.
📚 References
Video: 9 Common Marking Mistakes & Fixes (Kurt Gibson) Video: Physical Downfield Defense (Everything Ultimate)
M3

Offensive Systems

▼
  • Horizontal Stack (H-Stack): Creating wide isolation lanes for middle cutters and exploiting deep space.
  • Give-and-Go Flow: Immediate follow-through cuts by handlers after releasing a pass to keep the defense shifting.
  • End-Zone Offense: Tight-space isolations, front-of-stack clears, and high-percentage score options.
📚 References
Video: First Person Ultimate w/ Thoughts (cchanulti) Film Study: USA vs Belgium — U-24 Worlds Final
M4

Athleticism & Game Conditioning

▼
  • Footwork Drills: Ladder drills for lateral quickness, deceleration mechanics, and rapid pivot transitions.
  • Sprint Repeatability: High-intensity interval training (HIIT) mimicking the fast-rest-fast work-to-rest ratio of live points.
📚 References
Video: Throw 75 Feet Further — Basics (Latitude 64) KM Ultimate Tuesday Sessions — S&C with Jari Villanueva
Program 03 · Elite Execution & Mental Mastery

Elite Program

Target Audience: National team candidates, high-tier club captains, and elite competitive players.

Core Objective: High-speed decision-making, physical dominance, situational tactical mastery, and leadership.

M1

High-Velocity & Situational Throwing

▼
  • Full-Field Hucks: Releasing 50+ yard hucks under intense pressure with precise touch, flight-path control, and edge stability.
  • High-Pressure Releases: Off-axis throws, Scoobers, Blades, High Releases, and inverted passes under mark pressure.
  • Micro-Fakes: Using eye level, shoulder twitches, and pivot adjustments to manipulate mark hips without throwing.
📚 References
Video: Huck A Backhand FAR (Kurt Gibson) Video: Huck A Forehand FAR (Kurt Gibson) Video: High Release Flick (Eric Deng) Video: The Scoober (Rowan McDonnell) Video: The Hammer (Brodie Smith)
M2

Tactical Flexibility & In-Game Adjustments

▼
  • Matchup-Based Offense: Real-time adaptation to defensive switches, zone-to-person transitions, and wind shifts.
  • Junk & Transition Defenses: Disrupty defenses (e.g., "Person out of Zone" or "Zone to Man on pass 3") designed to drain stall counts and force low-percentage deep shots.
  • Counter-Tactics: Beating high-pressure poaching, bracket traps, and physical double-teaming in the backfield.
📚 References
Video: How To Beat ANY Mark (Kurt Gibson) Video: Avoid These 4 Forehand Mistakes (Kurt Gibson)
M3

High-Performance Athletic Training

▼
  • Plyometrics & Vertical Jump: Power production for layout catches, sky contests, and explosive multi-directional launches.
  • Injury Prevention (Prehab): Hamstring strengthening, ankle mobility, hip flexor stability, and shoulder cuff maintenance.
📚 References
Consult: Jari Villanueva — S&C Head, KM Ultimate Training Committee
M4

Elite Mindset & On-Field Leadership

▼
  • In-Game Analytics: Tracking completion ratios, turn points, and high-value turnover sectors to refine strategy mid-game.
  • Clutch Performance: Maintaining composure under cap-point pressure, fatigue management, and managing game pacing.
  • Leadership & Communication: Delivering concise sidestack feedback, calling effective line-substitutions, and maintaining positive team culture.
