01 / Welcome to the Family

Welcome to Kid Mafia

Whether you're holding a disc for the first time or chasing an elite level of play — you belong here.

Kid Mafia is more than a frisbee team — we are a community dedicated to developing Ultimate Frisbee players across Laguna. Whether you are just picking up a disc for the first time or looking to sharpen elite skills, this organization exists to support your growth on and off the field.

Everything you need is here

This site is home base: our mission and values, our training schedule and venue, and the people who lead the family — plus a full beginner's playbook for anyone picking up a disc for the first time. Explore each section and reach out through our socials anytime.

Play hard. Play fair. Lift others up.
02 / Who We Are

About Kid Mafia

Built in Laguna, open to everyone. Here's what drives us.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, high-quality training, mentorship, and competitive opportunities for Ultimate Frisbee players living in Laguna — helping them develop technical skill, tactical knowledge, physical fitness, and strong character.

Our Vision

A thriving Ultimate community in Laguna where every player — regardless of experience level — has the support, coaching, and pathway needed to reach their full potential.

Our Goals

  • Develop skilled, knowledgeable, and spirited Ultimate players in Laguna.
  • Offer regular, high-quality training open to members of all levels.
  • Cater clinics and outreach programs to schools and universities across Laguna, introducing and growing the sport at the institutional level.
  • Build a supportive network where experienced players mentor and uplift newer athletes.
  • Foster a positive reputation for Kid Mafia and for Ultimate Frisbee throughout the province.

Our Values

Family First

We treat every member with respect and support. No one is left behind.

Spirit of the Game

We compete fiercely while upholding integrity, honesty, and sportsmanship.

Growth Mindset

Every practice and every game is an opportunity to improve.

Community Impact

We actively help other players in Laguna raise their level of play.

Accountability

We show up on time, prepared, and ready to contribute.
